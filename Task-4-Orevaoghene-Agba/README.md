# Sales Dashboard Visualization – Revenue Leakage Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on data visualization and dashboard storytelling using Excel to communicate sales performance, customer behaviour, and revenue leakage insights through interactive charts and KPI cards.

The dashboard was designed to support executive-level decision-making by presenting key business metrics in a clear and visually engaging format.

---

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- PivotCharts
- KPI Cards
- Slicers
- Dashboard Design Techniques

---

## Dashboard Features
- Gross sales KPIs
- Completed vs unrealized revenue comparison
- Revenue by referral channel
- Product performance analysis
- Order status analysis
- Sales trend analysis
- Revenue leakage insights

---

## Workbook Structure
- Dashboards
- Key Findings & Recommendations
- Descriptive_Statistics_Analysis
- Pivot_Tables
- Cleaned Dataset
- Raw Dataset

---

## Key Insights
- Gross sales generated from all transactions totaled $1.26m
- Completed sales accounted for only $488.8k, while unrealized sales reached $776.0k.
- Revenue leakage reduced the organization’s ability to convert gross revenue into realized income.
- High cancellation and pending orders contributed to major revenue leakage.
- Referral channels such as Instagram and Email appear more effective for revenue generation. 

---

## Recommendations
- Follow up on pending orders faster to reduce unrealized revenue.
- Improve customer retention and satisfaction strategies to reduce order returns.
- Increase focus on high-performing referral channels.
